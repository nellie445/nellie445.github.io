Data Analytics Internship

Cultural Arts Society of West Jordan (CASWJ) • 2026

Supported a nonprofit arts organization by cleaning, analyzing, and preparing operational ticketing data for reporting, exploratory analysis, and reusable visualization workflows.

Python Jupyter Notebook pandas NumPy Excel Google Sheets Looker Studio Data Cleaning EDA

Internship Overview

I completed a data analytics internship with the Cultural Arts Society of West Jordan (CASWJ) as part of a WGU student team. The broader engagement focused on strengthening the organization's data collection, analysis, reporting, and longer-term data systems.

My primary work centered on the ticketing and attendance workstream using BuyYourTix data associated with Sugar Factory Playhouse and Youth Theatre. I developed reproducible data preparation and exploratory-analysis workflows and prepared outputs that could support organizational reporting and visualization.

My Contribution

  • Reviewed ticketing datasets for structure, quality, inconsistencies, and analytical readiness.
  • Built Python and Jupyter Notebook workflows for repeatable data cleaning and exploratory analysis.
  • Standardized fields and data types, including dates, customer names, and production names.
  • Preserved raw source data while producing separate cleaned and analysis-ready outputs.
  • Generated inspection reports, data-quality outputs, analysis tables, and structured Excel deliverables.
  • Analyzed attendance, productions, ticket categories, transaction codes, monthly trends, and booking lead times.
  • Prepared data and visualizations for reporting workflows using Google Sheets and Looker Studio.
  • Documented methodology and retained original source labels where business definitions still required confirmation.

Data Preparation Workflow

The analysis began with preserving the original CSV data and inspecting the datasets before transformation. Cleaning was performed separately so that source files remained intact and the process could be reproduced.

Workflow

Raw ticketing data → Inspection & quality review → Cleaning & standardization → Exploratory analysis → Reporting-ready outputs → Dashboard visualization

The resulting workflow produced cleaned datasets along with inspection, quality, analytical, and Excel outputs. This separation made it easier to validate transformations and trace analysis results back to the underlying data.

Exploratory Data Analysis

I used Jupyter Notebook as the primary environment for validation, calculations, exploratory analysis, and methodology documentation. The analysis examined multiple dimensions of the organization's ticketing activity.

  • Attendance summaries and attendance by production.
  • Monthly attendance trends.
  • Ticket-type and ticket-category distributions.
  • Transaction-code summaries.
  • Booking lead-time analysis.
  • Review of free, discounted, and complimentary ticket activity.

Reporting & Visualization

After validating calculations in Python, I worked with structured outputs that could be recreated and visualized through Google Sheets and Looker Studio. This supported a transition from one-time exploratory analysis toward reporting that could be reused by the organization.

Dashboard work included views for attendance by production, monthly trends, ticket categories, and transaction codes, along with summary metrics and filtering concepts for production and date ranges.

Working With Business Definitions

An important part of the project was recognizing where the data alone could not answer a business-definition question. For example, some ticketing fields required organizational confirmation before they could safely be interpreted as issued tickets, redeemed tickets, or physical attendance.

Rather than silently imposing assumptions, I preserved original labels and documented areas that required confirmation. This helped separate verified calculations from interpretations that depended on organizational context.
